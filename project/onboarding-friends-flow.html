Loose Days · interactive prototype

Getting in, and finding your people, without the dead ends

A working click-through of a reworked sign-up and friend search. Onboarding becomes three light steps you can skip through instead of one gated screen, and the two separate lookup boxes collapse into a single search that takes a name, an email, or a number. Everything below is live — type in it, tap things.

    What changes, point for point

    Moment Today Proposed
    First screen after sign-in One screen holding a phone field, a contact importer, a number lookup, an email lookup and a Continue button. Three steps with a progress bar: identify yourself, find people, mark a few days. One decision per screen.
    Getting past onboarding Hard-gated on saving a phone number. No number, no app — and pressing Save is a separate manual step that silently un-saves if you edit the field again. Number saves itself when it's valid, with an inline tick. Every step has a real “Skip for now” that lands you in the app.
    Why you're handing over a number Unexplained. The hashing guarantee lives in the README, not in front of the person typing. Said at the field: stored as a one-way hash, never in the clear, never shown to anyone.
    Finding one person Two boxes with two models — “add by number” and “find by email” — and you have to know which one applies before you can start. One box. Type a name, an email or a number; the field works out which it is and searches all three.
    Searching by name Not possible. Exact number or exact email only — you need the data before you can look anyone up. Name search across friends-of-friends, which is where nearly everyone you'd add actually is.
    Arriving with nothing A blank screen and a search box. Nothing to act on until you produce a number from memory. Suggestions are on screen before you type — mutual friends first, then anyone from your contacts already here.
    People not on Loose Days Split: numbers queue for a bulk SMS, emails fire one at a time from a different box. One queue, one tally, one hand-off — text and email invites go out together from the same button.
    Landing in the app Empty calendar, empty friends list, nothing scheduled. Friends already listed, invites ready to send, and a fortnight of days you marked on the way in.

    What it takes to build

    • GET /api/friends/suggestions Friends-of-friends, ranked by mutual count. Two hops over the existing friend_edges table, which is already symmetric — no new data collected, no new columns.
    • POST /api/friends/search One endpoint behind the single box. Detects email / phone / name, then folds together today's search_by_email and match_contacts plus a name match. Name matching is scoped to friends-of-friends on purpose — a global name directory would leak the whole user list, so a stranger stays unfindable by name.
    • <FriendSearch /> Replaces ContactFinder and EmailFinder. Both current components already ship on two screens each; one component keeps onboarding and /friends/add identical for free.
    • /onboarding?step= Three steps in the existing route, step held in the URL so back works. Auto-save on the phone field removes the stale-save trap in the current screen, and completeOnboarding() becomes reachable from any step.
    • invite queue Merged hand-off. Email invites go through inviteEmail, numbers through the existing bulk SMS link and shared createInviteLink token — one button, same two mechanisms underneath.
    Prototype only — mock data, nothing here talks to the backend. Colours and spacing are taken from src/routes/layout.css; the app's Space Grotesk / Inter pairing falls back to system faces here because artifact pages can't load webfonts.
